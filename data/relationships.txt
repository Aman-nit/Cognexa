### REL001
Relationship ID: REL001
From: insured -> To: policy
Cardinality: one-to-many

A policyholder can hold one or more policies.

---
Join condition (for SQL generation only):
insured.insured_id = policy.insured_id

### REL002
Relationship ID: REL002
From: policy -> To: vehicle
Cardinality: one-to-many

A policy covers one or more vehicles.

---
Join condition (for SQL generation only):
policy.policy_number = vehicle.policy_number

### REL003
Relationship ID: REL003
From: policy -> To: incident
Cardinality: one-to-many

A policy can have one or more incidents reported against it.

---
Join condition (for SQL generation only):
policy.policy_number = incident.policy_number

### REL004
Relationship ID: REL004
From: incident -> To: claim
Cardinality: one-to-one

Each incident generates exactly one claim record.

---
Join condition (for SQL generation only):
incident.incident_id = claim.incident_id
