### BR001
Rule ID: BR001
Category: fraud
Name: High claim relative to premium
Severity: warning

A claim amount more than 40x the policyholder's annual premium is disproportionate and warrants closer review of the incident details.

---
Condition (for rule engine only, not for semantic search):
claim.total_claim_amount > (policy.policy_annual_premium * 40)

### BR002
Rule ID: BR002
Category: fraud
Name: Early claim after policy start
Severity: warning

A high-value claim filed within the first 3 months of a policy is a recognized pattern associated with intent-to-defraud at signup.

---
Condition (for rule engine only, not for semantic search):
policy.months_as_customer < 3 AND claim.total_claim_amount > 30000

### BR003
Rule ID: BR003
Category: fraud
Name: No witnesses on a multi-party incident
Severity: info

Zero witnesses on an incident involving other parties or vehicles reduces independent verification and raises investigative priority.

---
Condition (for rule engine only, not for semantic search):
incident.witnesses = 0 AND incident.incident_type != 'Parked Car'

### BR004
Rule ID: BR004
Category: fraud
Name: High severity but no authorities contacted
Severity: warning

Major damage or total loss incidents are ordinarily reported to some authority (police, fire, ambulance). Absence of any contact is inconsistent with the claimed severity.

---
Condition (for rule engine only, not for semantic search):
incident.incident_severity IN ('Major Damage', 'Total Loss') AND incident.authorities_contacted IS NULL

### BR005
Rule ID: BR005
Category: underwriting
Name: No police report expected for theft claims
Severity: info

For vehicle theft claims specifically, a missing police report is a known and expected gap in this dataset and should not, by itself, be treated as suspicious.

---
Condition (for rule engine only, not for semantic search):
incident.incident_type = 'Vehicle Theft' AND incident.police_report_available = 'NO'

### BR006
Rule ID: BR006
Category: fraud
Name: Property damage reported with zero property claim
Severity: warning

Reported property damage with no corresponding property claim amount is an internal inconsistency worth flagging for review.

---
Condition (for rule engine only, not for semantic search):
incident.property_damage = 'YES' AND claim.property_claim = 0

### BR007
Rule ID: BR007
Category: fraud
Name: Bodily injury reported with zero injury claim
Severity: warning

Bodily injuries recorded on the incident but no injury claim amount is an inconsistency between the incident record and the claim record.

---
Condition (for rule engine only, not for semantic search):
incident.bodily_injuries > 0 AND claim.injury_claim = 0

### BR008
Rule ID: BR008
Category: underwriting
Name: High umbrella limit policy
Severity: info

Policies with an umbrella limit of $5,000,000 or more carry higher exposure and should be reviewed with additional scrutiny regardless of claim outcome.

---
Condition (for rule engine only, not for semantic search):
policy.umbrella_limit >= 5000000

### BR009
Rule ID: BR009
Category: escalation
Name: High-value claim warning threshold
Severity: warning

This threshold corresponds to the 90th percentile of total claim amounts in the historical dataset. Claims above it are flagged for supervisory review regardless of other rule outcomes.

---
Condition (for rule engine only, not for semantic search):
claim.total_claim_amount > 81400

### BR010
Rule ID: BR010
Category: underwriting
Name: Aging vehicle high-value claim
Severity: warning

A high vehicle-damage claim on a vehicle older than 15 years at time of incident is disproportionate to typical depreciated vehicle value and warrants valuation review.

---
Condition (for rule engine only, not for semantic search):
(2015 - vehicle.auto_year) > 15 AND claim.vehicle_claim > 40000

### BR011
Rule ID: BR011
Category: escalation
Name: Escalate to human review
Severity: escalation

Two or more independently triggered fraud-category rules on the same claim moves the recommendation from a system-generated warning to a mandatory human investigator review. The system never auto-decides fraud; it only ever recommends escalation.

---
Condition (for rule engine only, not for semantic search):
TRIGGERED_RULE_COUNT(category='fraud') >= 2
